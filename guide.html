Advanced

Skill Range: Gold to Platinum Ranks

Gold and Platinum players are very skilled with both offence and defence, and are able to easily do long skill chains. Focus on mastering advanced defense techniques and panel management.

Expert

Skill Range: Diamond, Master, and Grandmaster Ranks

Players at this level are comfortable battling on the highest difficulty level for extended periods of time, effectively dishing out powerful offence and surviving while topped out. Focus on optimizing playstyle, practicing high level techniques (factory, transition clear, shogun), and learning to survive in increasingly difficult situations.
