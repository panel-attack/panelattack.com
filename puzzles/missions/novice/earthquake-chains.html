Novice - Earthquake Chains

My favorite trick to watch. There is a shelf of panels sitting over a match; clear the match and the whole shelf drops a level, landing beside a third of its color. The chain spans two heights of the board at once - hence the name. The second half of this set does it from higher up, with further to fall.
