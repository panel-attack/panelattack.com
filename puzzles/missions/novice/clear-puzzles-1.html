Novice - Clear Puzzles 1

Garbage! The big slabs are blocks sent by an opponent, and they only leave your board one way: match panels next to them and they turn into ordinary panels for you to use. That is called a clear. Making a match is not the hard part here - making it in the one row that touches the block is.
