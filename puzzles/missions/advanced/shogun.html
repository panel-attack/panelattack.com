Advanced - Shogun

A tier is a layer of panels above or between garbage. A shogun is when a panel from an upper tier falls and chains after the garbage beneath it is cleared - so you put the panel up there before you break the block, and breaking it hands you a chain for free. Ten boards of arranging the tier above first.
